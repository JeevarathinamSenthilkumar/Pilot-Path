## Editorial limitations
Each lesson is concise and source-page linked but this educational version is not certified flight instruction or a replacement for FAA regulations, AIM, ACS, or an aircraft flight manual. The chapter is from FAA-H-8083-25C; the handbook can describe historical rules or administrative systems that have since changed. Custom diagrams are conceptual teaching aids, not FAA originals.

A chart or figure can sometimes contain dense labels; use zoom and read the accompanying app explanation. Figure ownership is labeled next to each illustration.
